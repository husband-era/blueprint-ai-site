The Solution

Three AI systems.
Fully automated operations.

Each system solves one of your core bottlenecks. They work together, run 24/7, and require zero manual input.

System 01

Auto-Send Order Links

When a new drop goes live on Brandboom, your entire buyer list gets the ordering link automatically — via SMS and email, personalized, in seconds.

  • Triggered instantly by new Brandboom collections
  • Personalized SMS + email to every buyer
  • Buyers get the link in seconds, not hours
System 02

Smart Follow-Up Sequences

AI tracks who ordered and who didn't, then automatically follows up at 24h, 48h, and 72h with the right message for each buyer.

  • Automated 24h / 48h / 72h follow-up cadence
  • Different messaging for buyers vs. no-shows
  • Flags unresponsive buyers for your personal outreach
System 03

AI Order Dashboard

Every Brandboom order auto-populates into a clean, organized dashboard in real time. No manual input — ever.

  • Real-time sync from Brandboom → live dashboard
  • Buyer, items, payment status, shipping — all tracked
  • Weekly AI summary report texted to your phone
